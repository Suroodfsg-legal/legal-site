SURO · NIGHT HARBOR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Suro iOS application (“Suro”) handles information. The bundle identifier is com.suro.odfsg. The harbor desk contact is night.desk@suro.app.

1. Information in your profile

When you tap Quick Login, Suro creates a display name and an access token and keeps them with your profile, together with a record that you accepted the 18+ confirmation.

Your profile can also hold:

  • Tonight’s vibe, a short “Tonight I…” line, stealth mode, and the vanish-at-dawn setting
  • Lamp balance and lamp ledger entries
  • Waves, chats, night-log captions, and room messages you write
  • Voice files you record (a wave, a 15-second intro, a station queue clip, or a room tone)
  • Photos and videos you capture or choose for a portrait, a night log, or a chat still
  • People you block and posts you remove from your feed
  • Whether the notification prompt has already been shown

Suro does not ask you for a legal name, phone number, or payment card.

2. Permissions

Suro asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it.

  • Microphone: when you record a wave, a voice intro, a station queue clip, a room tone, or a chat ripple.
  • Camera: when you capture a night photo or video, or a harbor portrait.
  • Photo Library: when you attach an existing photo or video to a night log, a portrait, or a conversation.
  • Speech Recognition: when you turn on dictation for a vibe line or a wave.
  • Notifications: the system notification prompt appears the first time you open the harbor after login. Suro asks for this permission and does not send chat or marketing alerts through it.

You can change these choices in iOS Settings. Suro does not include a separate permission-management screen.

3. Purchases

Lamp packs are purchased with Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. Suro receives the product identifier and adds the matching lamp count to your balance. Suro does not receive your card number. Product identifiers are com.suro.lampsone, com.suro.lampstwo, com.suro.lampsthree, com.suro.lampsfour, com.suro.lampsfive, com.suro.lampssix, and com.suro.lampsseven.

4. Facial data

4.1 What we collect

Suro does not collect biometric faceprints, face geometry, or facial recognition templates. If you take or choose a photo or video for a night log, a chat still, or your harbor portrait, that media may depict a face. The photo or video is the media you selected. It is not converted into a biometric identifier.

4.2 How we use facial data

A photo or video that may depict a face is used only to display that media inside Suro: as your harbor portrait, inside a night log, or inside a conversation you attach it to. Suro does not use photos or videos to identify you, to match faces across people, or to train a recognition model.

4.3 Disclosure, sharing, and where photos and videos are stored

Suro does not sell photos or videos and does not send them to a third-party artificial intelligence provider. A portrait, night-log photo or video, or chat still is stored in the Suro documents folder on the iPhone where you created it. A report email you choose to send includes a text snippet. It does not attach the media file unless you add one yourself in Mail.

4.4 Retention

Photos and videos remain with your Suro profile until you replace the portrait, delete the post, or delete your account. Delete Account removes the Suro documents folder on that iPhone, including portraits, night photos, night videos, and chat stills.

This chapter is the explanation of Suro’s collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention of facial data.

5. Dictation and third-party AI

When you turn on dictation, Suro uses Apple Speech Recognition for the words you speak into a vibe line or a wave. Apple may process that dictation audio under Apple’s terms. Suro does not send the audio to any other company.

Suro does not send your prompts, night notes, voice recordings, photos, or videos to a third-party artificial intelligence service for generation or analysis. No reference image or video is uploaded for generation. Suro does not offer an image-generation or video-generation feature.

6. Pocket Radio

Pocket Radio plays public streams from stations such as SomaFM, KEXP, and FIP. Opening a stream sends a normal audio request to that station. Suro does not attach your profile name, notes, photos, or videos to the request. The station can see a network connection the way any audio client connection is visible to it.

7. Community safety

Suro checks night notes, waves, chat messages, and room messages against a phrase list before they are published. A blocked phrase is refused.

Report, from the more menu, opens Mail to night.desk@suro.app. The message contains the author’s display name, the reason you chose (harassment, inappropriate audio, commercial spam, or underage account), and a short text snippet, and it asks the harbor desk to remove the content and eject that person within 24 hours. Choosing Report also places that person on your block list so their harbor, waves, and night log leave your view.

Block asks you to confirm, then keeps that person on the Quiet List until you unblock them. Remove from feed hides that wave or night-log post from your feed immediately. Contact Developer in Harbor Settings opens Mail to the same address.

Suro does not sell personal information.

8. Children

Suro is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used Suro, contact night.desk@suro.app.

9. Deletion and logout

Leave Harbor (Log Out) ends the session and keeps the profile so Quick Login can be used again.

Delete Account, after you confirm the warning, erases the Suro profile domain and the app documents folder on that iPhone. That includes the generated credentials, lamp balance, ledger, notes, block list, voice files, photos, and videos.

Removing the app from the iPhone also removes the profile stored with the app.
